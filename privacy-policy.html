ElectriCalc - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

Overview

ElectriCalc ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by Trade Calc Suite. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use ElectriCalc.

Data Collection

ElectriCalc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The App operates entirely offline. All calculations, history, and settings are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server or third party.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No device identifiers
  • No browsing or search history
  • No health, financial, or biometric data

Local Data Storage

ElectriCalc stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Calculation history: Your saved calculations are stored using Apple's SwiftData framework on your device.
  • User preferences: Settings such as preferred electrical standard (NEC/CEC), default voltage, appearance, and PDF export preferences are stored in UserDefaults on your device.
  • Business information: If you choose to add company name, license number, or logo for PDF exports, this data is stored locally on your device only.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Subscriptions

ElectriCalc offers auto-renewable subscriptions managed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not process or store any payment information. All subscription transactions are handled by Apple. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles payment data.

Third-Party Services

ElectriCalc does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. The only third-party service involved is Apple's StoreKit for processing in-app subscriptions.

Children's Privacy

ElectriCalc does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is a professional tool designed for licensed electricians and electrical professionals.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by deleting the ElectriCalc app from your device. No data exists on any server to be deleted.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
